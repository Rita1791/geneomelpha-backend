# Research and Clinical Limitations

Geneomelpha is a proof-of-concept bioinformatics communication platform. It is designed for research, education, demonstration, and translational interface exploration.

It is not a clinical product.

---

## What Geneomelpha Supports

Geneomelpha supports:

- genomic literacy
- patient-friendly report explanation
- educational visualization
- translational bioinformatics demonstration
- prototype clinical-genomics communication workflows
- user-interface exploration for genomic report communication

---

## What Geneomelpha Does Not Claim

Geneomelpha does not claim:

- clinical diagnosis
- medical advice
- treatment recommendation
- clinical decision support
- regulatory approval
- clinical validation
- replacement of genetic counseling
- replacement of certified laboratory interpretation
- replacement of clinician review
- validated risk prediction
- validated pharmacogenomic recommendation
- direct interpretation of real patient data for medical decision-making

---

## Intended Use

The platform is intended for:

| Use Case | Status |
|---|---|
| Educational demonstration | Supported |
| Research communication | Supported |
| Prototype UI/UX exploration | Supported |
| Clinical decision support | Not supported |
| Diagnosis or treatment | Not supported |
| Real patient reporting | Not supported without full clinical validation |

---

## Required Future Work Before Clinical Use

Before any clinical or real-world healthcare deployment, Geneomelpha would require:

1. expert clinical review
2. genetic counselor review
3. validation against real-world genomic reports
4. validation across different report formats
5. privacy and security review
6. regulatory and ethical assessment
7. usability testing with clinicians and patients
8. documentation of evidence sources
9. audit trail for interpretation changes
10. review by certified clinical genomics professionals

---

## Data and Privacy Boundary

This repository should not contain:

- real patient genomic reports
- identifiable health information
- private clinical data
- production credentials
- private company data
- confidential laboratory reports
- real medical decision outputs

Only demonstration data, educational examples, and non-sensitive prototype material should be included.

---

## Final Boundary Statement

Geneomelpha is a research and educational prototype for improving genomic communication. It must not be used as a diagnostic tool, treatment advisor, or substitute for clinician or genetic counselor review.
